# 13. Closing Packet

Every appointment ends with a handoff of the work, a closing letter, and a short exit survey. This packet keeps the project usable after the member leaves and gives the member a record of what they did.

## Part A. Exit checklist (member)

Complete these steps in the last week of the appointment.

- [ ] Submit the deliverable named in the trainee agreement.
- [ ] Push all code to the project repository and confirm that it runs from the top.
- [ ] Update the project README and codebook with anything you added or changed.
- [ ] Update your entries in the contribution record (`docs/contributions.md`).
- [ ] Write a one-page handoff note: what you did, where the files are, what is unfinished, and what you would do next.
- [ ] Delete any local copies of licensed or confidential data and confirm this in writing to your supervisor.
- [ ] Return any equipment or access cards.
- [ ] Complete the closing letter request (Part C).
- [ ] Complete the exit survey ([Document 14](14-survey-instruments.md)).

## Part B. Exit checklist (supervisor)

- [ ] Review the deliverable and handoff note.
- [ ] Confirm that data has been deleted and remove access to restricted folders.
- [ ] Remove the member from the repository, or change their role to read-only if they remain a co-author.
- [ ] Write and send the closing letter.
- [ ] Add the member to the lab's alumni page, with their consent.

## Part C. Closing letter request

The member fills in this form and sends it with the handoff note.

---

**Name:**
**Project:**
**Appointment period:**
**Hours per week (average):**

**Main tasks.** List three to five tasks you carried out.

**Deliverable.** Describe the deliverable and where it is saved.

**Skills used.** List the methods, software, and data you worked with.

**Outputs.** Any paper, presentation, or dataset that used your work.

**Purpose of the letter.** A general record of the appointment, or a letter for a specific application (graduate school, job)? If specific, give the program or employer and the deadline.

---

## Part D. Closing letter template (supervisor)

> [Date]
>
> To whom it may concern,
>
> [Name] worked in the [lab or hub name] at the University of Toronto as a research trainee from [start date] to [end date], under my supervision, on the project [project title].
>
> The project [one or two sentences on the question and data]. [Name]'s role was to [main tasks]. Over the appointment, [Name] [specific evidence of the quality of the work, such as a dataset built, an error found, a section drafted, or a seminar presentation given].
>
> [Name] completed [deliverable]. [This work is acknowledged in / contributed to] [output, if any].
>
> Sincerely,
>
> [Supervisor name, title, and contact]

The closing letter is a record of the appointment. A recommendation letter for graduate school or a job is a separate document. The supervisor decides whether to write one based on the member's work and the lab's letter policy.
