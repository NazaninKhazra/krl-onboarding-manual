# 13. Closing Packet

Every appointment ends with a handoff of the work, a closing letter, and a short exit survey. This packet keeps the project usable after the member leaves and gives the member a record of what they did. Lab manuals in other fields treat offboarding as a formal step for the same reasons (Andreev et al., 2022).

## Part A. Exit checklist (member)

Complete these steps in the last week of the appointment.

- [ ] Submit the deliverable named in the project description.
- [ ] Push all code to the project repository and confirm that it runs from the top.
- [ ] Update the project README and codebook with anything you added or changed.
- [ ] Update your entries in the contribution record (`docs/contributions.md`).
- [ ] Write a one-page handoff note: what you did, where the files are, what is unfinished, and what you would do next.
- [ ] Delete any local copies of licensed or confidential data and confirm this in writing to your supervisor.
- [ ] Complete the closing letter request (Part C).
- [ ] Complete the exit survey ([Document 14](14-survey-instruments.md)).

## Part B. Exit checklist (supervisor)

- [ ] Review the deliverable and handoff note.
- [ ] Confirm that data has been deleted and remove access to restricted folders.
- [ ] Remove the member from the repository, or change their role to read-only if they remain a co-author.
- [ ] Write and send the closing letter within two weeks.
- [ ] Add the member to the lab's alumni page, with their consent.

## Part C. Closing letter request (member)

Send this form with the handoff note.

---

**Name:**
**Project:**
**Appointment period:**
**Average hours per week:**

**Main tasks.** List three to five tasks you carried out.

**Deliverable.** Describe the deliverable and where it is saved.

**Skills used.** List the methods, software, and data you worked with.

**Outputs.** Any paper, presentation, or dataset that used your work.

---

## Part D. Closing letter template (supervisor)

> [Date]
>
> To whom it may concern,
>
> [Name] worked in the [lab or hub name] at the University of Toronto as a student research trainee from [start date] to [end date], under my supervision, on the project [project title].
>
> The project [one or two sentences on the question and data]. [Name]'s role was to [main tasks]. During the appointment, [Name] [specific evidence of the quality of the work, such as a dataset built, an error found, a section drafted, or a seminar talk given].
>
> [Name] completed [deliverable]. [This work is acknowledged in / contributed to] [output, if any].
>
> Sincerely,
>
> [Supervisor name, title, and contact]

## Closing letters and recommendation letters

The closing letter is a record of the appointment. Every member who completes the deliverable receives one.

A recommendation letter for graduate school or a job is a separate document. Undergraduates who worked in K.R.L. are eligible for recommendation letters from Prof. Khazra for graduate school and job applications. Letters are not written for other research assistant positions or internships, except at a small number of leading universities and employers. To request one, complete the request form, then email Prof. Khazra, and follow up if you have not heard back. Allow at least three weeks before the deadline.

Supervisors in other units should apply their own letter policy.
