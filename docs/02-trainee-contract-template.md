# 2. Research Trainee Agreement (Template)

This one-page agreement is signed by the trainee and the supervising professor at the start of each appointment. It sets the scope of the work, the time commitment, and what the trainee receives at the end. The template is written so that other units, such as the Vic Research Hub, can use it with minor edits.

Where a University of Toronto agreement form for unpaid research trainees applies, that form is the formal agreement. This document is then attached to it as the project description.

---

## Research Trainee Agreement

**Trainee name:** ____________________ **Student number:** ____________

**Program and year:** ____________________

**Supervising professor:** ____________________ **Department or unit:** ____________

**Project title:** ____________________

### 1. Appointment period

Start date: ____________ End date: ____________

The minimum appointment is two weeks. Either party may end the appointment early with one week's written notice.

### 2. Status

The trainee holds an unpaid research training position. The position is not employment. The trainee may receive course credit only if they are registered in a course that allows it (course code, if any: ____________).

### 3. Time commitment

Expected hours per week: ______ (should not exceed ______ hours in any week).

The trainee's coursework takes priority. The trainee should tell the supervisor in advance if a heavy academic week will reduce their hours.

### 4. Scope of work

The trainee will carry out the following tasks:

1. ________________________________________________
2. ________________________________________________
3. ________________________________________________

Tasks outside this list require the agreement of both parties.

### 5. Deliverable

At the end of the appointment, the trainee will submit:

________________________________________________

(for example, a cleaned dataset with documentation, a literature review of 8 to 10 pages, a section of a working paper, or a seminar presentation)

### 6. Supervision and meetings

The supervisor will meet the trainee every ______ (week / two weeks) for about ______ minutes. The trainee will submit a short weekly report before each meeting.

### 7. Credit and closing letter

The trainee will be acknowledged in any output that uses their work. Co-authorship follows the lab's authorship policy, which the trainee has read.

When the deliverable is complete, the supervisor will write a closing letter that describes the project, the trainee's tasks, the deliverable, and the skills the trainee used.

### 8. Data and confidentiality

The trainee will follow the data rules for the project, including any licence or ethics conditions. The trainee will not share project data, code, or unpublished results outside the project without the supervisor's permission.

### 9. Use of AI tools

The trainee has read the lab's AI-use policy and will follow it.

---

## Notes for supervisors

- Keep the scope narrow. A trainee who finishes early can take on a second task under a new agreement.
- Section 5 should name a concrete output. "Help with the project" is not a deliverable.
- Agree on the content of the closing letter at the start so that both sides know what is being measured.
