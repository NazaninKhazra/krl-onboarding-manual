# 2. Research Trainee Agreement

An unpaid trainee appointment at the University of Toronto has two parts. The first is the University's Student Research Trainee letter and acknowledgement, which sets the legal terms of the appointment. The second is a one-page project description, which sets out the work itself. Both are signed before the trainee starts.

Other units that adopt this manual, such as the Vic Research Hub, should obtain the current version of the University letter from their own department or college office and attach the project description in Part B.

## Part A. The University of Toronto Student Research Trainee letter

The department issues the letter under the supervisor's signature. It confirms the appointment dates, describes the training activities and the project, and states that the supervisor will provide guidance and mentorship. The trainee signs the attached acknowledgement and returns it by the start date.

By signing the acknowledgement, the trainee confirms that they understand the following terms.

- The position is an unpaid training opportunity.
- The trainee is protected against legal liability arising from their research activities under the University's general liability insurance program, on the same basis as employees.
- The trainee takes part on a voluntary basis. They are not covered by the Workplace Safety and Insurance Act, and the University does not provide health, disability, or accident insurance.
- The letter is not an offer of employment and does not give a right to future employment. The trainee is not a staff member and does not receive employee benefits.
- The trainee agrees to follow University policies that apply to people using University facilities. These include the Health and Safety Policy, the Provost's Guideline on Appropriate Use of Information and Communication Technology, the Copyright Policy, the Inventions Policy, and the policies on workplace harassment and workplace violence. Current versions are on the Governing Council website: <https://governingcouncil.utoronto.ca/secretariat/policies>.
- The trainee follows any departmental health and safety rules, which the department provides.

Questions about the letter go to the Department of Economics office.

## Part B. Project description

This page is attached to the University letter. It is completed by the supervisor and the trainee together at the first meeting.

**Supervising professor:** ____________________ **Department or unit:** ____________

**Project title:** ____________________

**Appointment period:** from ____________ to ____________ (minimum two weeks)

**Expected hours per week:** ______ (not more than ______ in any week)

### Tasks

1. ________________________________________________
2. ________________________________________________
3. ________________________________________________

Tasks outside this list need the agreement of both parties.

### Deliverable

At the end of the appointment, the trainee will submit:

________________________________________________

(for example, a cleaned dataset with a codebook, a literature review of 8 to 10 pages, a section of a working paper, or a seminar presentation)

### Supervision

The supervisor will meet the trainee every ______ (week / two weeks) for about ______ minutes. The trainee submits a weekly report before each meeting.

### Lab policies

The trainee has read and agrees to follow these documents in this manual:

- [ ] Expectations and Conduct ([Document 4](04-expectations-and-conduct.md))
- [ ] Data Ethics and Confidentiality ([Document 7](07-data-ethics-and-confidentiality.md))

### Ending the appointment

The appointment ends on the date above or when the deliverable is complete. Either party may end it earlier with one week's written notice. Coursework takes priority, and the trainee should tell the supervisor in advance if a heavy academic week will reduce their hours.

### Closing letter

When the deliverable is complete, the supervisor will write a closing letter describing the project, the trainee's tasks, the deliverable, and the skills used ([Document 13](13-closing-packet.md)).

## Notes for supervisors

- Keep the scope narrow. A trainee who finishes early can take on a second task under a new project description.
- The deliverable should name a concrete output. "Help with the project" does not tell the trainee what to produce or tell a reader what the trainee did.
- Agree on what the closing letter will describe at the start, so that both sides know how the work will be recorded.
- The Vic Research Hub proposal explains why the project description matters. In K.R.L.'s first-year surveys, the change members most wanted was clearer goals and scope.
