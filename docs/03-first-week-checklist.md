# 3. First-Week Checklist

Complete these steps in your first two weeks. Tick each item and send the completed list to your supervisor at the end of week 2.

Written onboarding steps help new members settle in faster and reduce misunderstandings about what is expected (Andreev et al., 2022). This checklist follows that advice and borrows the idea of a short practice task from the Gentzkow–Shapiro lab manual.

## Before you start

- [ ] Sign and return the University of Toronto Student Research Trainee letter, or your hiring letter ([Document 2](02-trainee-contract-template.md)).
- [ ] Complete the project description (Part B of Document 2) with your supervisor.
- [ ] Read [Document 1](01-welcome.md) (Welcome) and [Document 4](04-expectations-and-conduct.md) (Expectations and Conduct).
- [ ] Send your supervisor your preferred email, your GitHub username, and your weekly availability.

## Accounts and access

- [ ] Join the lab's Microsoft Teams group.
- [ ] Accept the invitation to the KRLResearchLab GitHub organization.
- [ ] Get access to the project folder named by your supervisor.
- [ ] Get access to the project data. For licensed or restricted data, sign the data-use terms first ([Document 7](07-data-ethics-and-confidentiality.md)).
- [ ] If your project uses data about people, complete the TCPS 2: CORE-2022 tutorial (<https://tcps2core.ca/>) and send the certificate to your supervisor.

## Software

- [ ] Install Python 3 (Anaconda or Miniconda) or R, as your project requires.
- [ ] Install Git and clone the project repository.
- [ ] Run the project's setup script or starter notebook and confirm that it works on your machine.
- [ ] Install Zotero (free) and join the project's shared reference library if there is one.

## Reading

- [ ] Read the project summary or the current draft of the paper.
- [ ] Read the two or three core papers your supervisor assigns. For each, write three or four sentences on the question, data, method, and main result.
- [ ] Read [Document 6](06-research-workflow-guide.md) (Research Workflow) and [Document 8](08-ai-use-policy.md) (AI-Use Policy).

## Practice task

- [ ] Complete the short practice task your supervisor assigns. A typical task is to reproduce one figure or table from the project's existing code, make a small change, and commit it to a new branch. The task checks that your setup works and introduces the project's code before you start on real work.

## First meetings

- [ ] Attend your first full-group meeting and introduce yourself.
- [ ] Meet your supervisor to agree on your first task and the date of your first weekly report.
- [ ] Meet your project team. Agree on how you will divide the work and where you will keep shared notes.
- [ ] Meet the returning member or graduate assistant who will be your first contact for practical questions.

## End of week 2

- [ ] Submit your first weekly report ([Document 5](05-weekly-report-template.md)).
- [ ] Write the research question of your project, as you understand it, in two or three sentences. Bring it to your next supervisor meeting.
