# 3. First-Week Checklist

Complete these steps in your first two weeks. Tick each item and send the completed list to your supervisor at the end of week 2.

## Before you start

- [ ] Read [Document 1](01-welcome.md) (Welcome) and [Document 4](04-expectations-and-conduct.md) (Expectations and Conduct).
- [ ] Sign the trainee agreement ([Document 2](02-trainee-contract-template.md)) or your hiring letter.
- [ ] Send your supervisor your preferred email, your GitHub username, and your weekly availability.

## Accounts and access

- [ ] Join the lab's Microsoft Teams channel.
- [ ] Accept the invitation to the KRLResearchLab GitHub organization.
- [ ] Get access to the project folder (OneDrive or the shared drive named by your supervisor).
- [ ] Get access to the project data. For licensed or restricted data, sign the data-use terms first ([Document 7](07-data-ethics-and-confidentiality.md)).
- [ ] If your project uses data about people, complete the TCPS 2: CORE-2022 tutorial and send your certificate to your supervisor.

## Software

- [ ] Install Python 3 (Anaconda or Miniconda) or R, as your project requires.
- [ ] Install Git and clone the project repository.
- [ ] Run the project's setup script or starter notebook and confirm that it works on your machine.
- [ ] Install a reference manager (Zotero is free) and join the project's shared library if there is one.

## Reading

- [ ] Read the project summary or the current draft of the paper.
- [ ] Read the two or three core papers your supervisor assigns. For each, write three or four sentences on the question, the data, the method, and the main result.
- [ ] Read [Document 6](06-research-workflow-guide.md) (Research Workflow) and [Document 8](08-ai-use-policy.md) (AI-Use Policy).

## First meetings

- [ ] Attend your first full-group meeting and introduce yourself and your project.
- [ ] Meet your supervisor to agree on your first task and the date of your first weekly report.
- [ ] Meet the other members of your project team. Agree on how you will divide the work and where you will keep shared notes.

## End of week 2

- [ ] Submit your first weekly report ([Document 5](05-weekly-report-template.md)).
- [ ] Write down, in two or three sentences, the research question of your project as you understand it. Bring it to your next supervisor meeting.
