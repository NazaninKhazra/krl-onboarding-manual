# 5. Weekly Report Template

Every member submits a short report each week. The report gives the supervisor a record of progress, brings problems to light early, and builds a written trail of the decisions behind the project. Reports are limited to two pages. Bullet points are fine.

Submit the report in the project folder as `reports/YYYY-MM-DD_lastname.md` (or `.docx`) by the day before the group meeting.

---

## Weekly Report

**Name:**
**Project:**
**Week of:**
**Hours worked this week:**

### 1. Goal for this week

The goal you set in last week's report, in one sentence.

### 2. What I did

List the tasks you completed. Link to the files, commits, or notebooks.

### 3. Results

Paste the main table or figure you produced, with two or three sentences on what it shows. If nothing worked, describe what you tried.

### 4. Problems

What blocked you or what you do not understand. Include exact error messages. Say what you have already tried.

### 5. Decisions

Any choice you made that affects the project (for example, how you handled missing values, which years you kept, which control group you used) and the reason for it.

### 6. Goal for next week

One main goal for next week. Add one or two smaller tasks if needed.

---

## Notes on writing a good report

- Section 3 matters most. A table with a short interpretation tells the supervisor more than a long list of tasks.
- Do not polish the report. It is a working document.
- If two reports in a row have the same goal, say why in Section 4. The supervisor will help you break the task down.
- Keep old reports. At the end of the appointment they become the record of your contribution.
