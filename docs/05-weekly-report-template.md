# 5. Weekly Report Template

Every member submits a short report each week. The report gives the supervisor a record of progress, brings problems to light while they can still be fixed, and builds a written trail of the decisions behind the project. Reports are limited to two pages. Bullet points are fine.

The format follows the weekly reports Prof. Khazra uses in her research courses (ECO225 and ECO1465), where a short weekly submission replaced large take-home assignments. A report trail is also hard to produce without doing the work, which matters when AI tools can produce a finished-looking file in minutes.

Save the report in the project folder as `reports/YYYY-MM-DD_lastname.md` (or `.docx`) by the day before the group meeting.

---

## Weekly Report

**Name:**
**Project:**
**Week of:**
**Hours worked this week:**

### 1. Goal for this week

The goal you set in last week's report, in one sentence.

### 2. What I did

List the tasks you completed. Link to the files, commits, or notebooks. Note any use of AI tools in one line ([Document 8](08-ai-use-policy.md)).

### 3. Results

Paste the main table or figure you produced, with two or three sentences on what it shows. If nothing worked, describe what you tried.

### 4. Problems

What blocked you or what you do not understand. Include exact error messages and what you have already tried.

### 5. Decisions

Any choice that affects the project (for example, how you handled missing values, which years you kept, or which control group you used) and the reason for it. Copy these into `docs/decisions.md`.

### 6. Goal for next week

One main goal for next week. Add one or two smaller tasks if needed.

---

## Notes for members

- Section 3 matters most. A table with a short interpretation tells the supervisor more than a long list of tasks.
- Do not polish the report. It is a working document.
- If two reports in a row have the same goal, explain why in Section 4. The supervisor will help you break the task into smaller steps.
- Keep your reports. They are the main record of your contribution when authorship is decided ([Document 11](11-authorship-and-credit.md)) and when your closing letter is written.

## Notes for supervisors

Feedback on a weekly report should be short and fast. Say whether the project is on a feasible path, whether the reasoning has moved since last week, and what the single priority is for next week. Chapter 12 of the Research Mentoring TA Manual gives examples of good weekly comments: <https://github.com/NazaninKhazra/research-mentoring-ta-manual/blob/main/manual/12-feedback-on-weekly-progress-reports.md>
