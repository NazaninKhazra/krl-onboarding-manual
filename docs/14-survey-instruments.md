# 14. Survey Instruments

The lab surveys members at the start and end of each appointment. The surveys show whether the program builds research skills and a sense of belonging in research, and they guide changes to the program. Using the same items across units, such as the Vic Research Hub, allows results to be compared.

Surveys are anonymous and voluntary. They take about 10 minutes. Responses are reported only in aggregate. If survey results will be used in a publication, the study must first receive ethics approval.

## Entry survey

**Background**

1. Year of study: 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5+ / MA / PhD
2. Program of study: ______
3. How did you hear about the lab? (course / website application / seminar / another student / other)
4. Have you worked on a research project before? (yes / no)

**Confidence in research skills**

Rate each item from 1 (not at all confident) to 5 (very confident).

5. Finding and reading academic papers in my field
6. Turning a broad topic into a research question
7. Cleaning and merging a dataset
8. Choosing a method that fits a research question
9. Interpreting regression results
10. Writing a section of a research paper
11. Presenting research to an audience

**Research identity**

Rate each item from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree).

12. I see myself as someone who can do research.
13. I feel I belong in a research setting.
14. I know what researchers in economics do day to day.
15. I am considering graduate study.

**Open questions**

16. What do you most hope to gain from this appointment?
17. What concerns you most about it?

## Exit survey

Repeat items 5 to 15 so that changes can be measured. Then add:

**Program features**

Rate how useful each feature was from 1 (not useful) to 5 (very useful), or "did not use."

18. Weekly group meetings
19. Weekly reports and feedback
20. Individual meetings with the supervisor
21. Working with other students on a team
22. Presenting at the seminar
23. Attending other members' seminar talks
24. The trainee agreement (clear scope and deliverable)
25. This manual

**Change in research identity**

26. If your sense of yourself as a researcher changed, what caused the change? Select all that apply.
    - Working with real data
    - Having my ideas shape the project
    - Feedback from the supervisor
    - Working with other students
    - Presenting my work
    - The prospect of co-authorship
    - Other: ______

**Open questions**

27. Describe a moment when you first felt like a researcher, if there was one.
28. What was the hardest part of the appointment?
29. What one change would most improve the program?
30. Would you recommend the program to another student? Why or why not?

## Administering the surveys

- Send the entry survey in week 1 and the exit survey in the last week of the appointment.
- Use Microsoft Forms through the University account so that data stays on University systems.
- Someone other than the direct supervisor should send reminders, to reduce pressure to respond.
