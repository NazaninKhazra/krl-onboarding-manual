# K.R.L. Onboarding Manual

Onboarding and training materials for research trainees and research assistants in the Khazra Research Lab (K.R.L.) for Applied Economics and Data Science, Department of Economics, University of Toronto.

The manual is written for new lab members and their supervisors. Several documents are written so that other units can adopt them with minor edits. They support the pilot of the Vic Research Hub (VRH) at Victoria College, which applies the K.R.L. model at the college level.

Documents marked "Shared core" apply to any faculty-supervised trainee program. The others refer to K.R.L.'s own tools, meetings, and methods, and can serve as models for other units.

## Using the manual

New members read Documents 1 to 8 in their first two weeks, following the checklist in Document 3. Documents 9 to 12 are references for later in the appointment. Document 13 is used at the end, and Document 17 describes what can follow.

Units adapting the manual should replace K.R.L.-specific names, links, and meeting schedules, and check Documents 2, 4, and 7 against their own institutional requirements.

## Companion manual for teaching assistants

The Research Mentoring TA Manual covers how to give feedback on student research: research questions, code, empirical results, and writing. It also includes a feedback simulation and runnable training cases. Supervisors and graduate assistants in the lab use it as their training guide.

## How this manual was built

The manual draws on K.R.L.'s first year (2025–26), including member surveys, and on the Vic Research Hub proposal. It also borrows from published lab manuals and guidance, which are cited in each document. The main sources are:

- Gentzkow and Shapiro's *Code and Data for the Social Sciences* and their lab's public RA manual, for project organization and the first-week practice task;
- Andreev et al. (2022) in *eLife*, on formal onboarding and offboarding in academic labs;
- the National Academies' report *The Science of Effective Mentorship in STEMM* (2019), on group mentoring;
- the Council on Undergraduate Research's *Characteristics of Excellence in Undergraduate Research* (2024), on program assessment;
- University of Toronto guidance on research ethics, research integrity, and generative AI.

Other public lab manuals that informed the structure include those of the Weecology lab (<https://lab-manual.weecology.org/>) and the Srikrishnan group at Cornell (<https://viveks.bee.cornell.edu/lab-manual>).
