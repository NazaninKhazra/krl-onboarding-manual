# K.R.L. Onboarding Manual

Onboarding and training materials for research trainees and research assistants in the Khazra Research Lab (K.R.L.) for Applied Economics and Data Science, Department of Economics, University of Toronto.

The manual is written for new lab members and their supervisors. Several documents are written so that other units can adopt them with minor edits. They are used in the pilot of the Vic Research Hub (VRH) at Victoria College, which applies the K.R.L. model at the college level.

Documents marked "Shared core" are written to apply to any faculty-supervised trainee program. The others refer to K.R.L.'s own tools, meetings, and methods, and can serve as models for other units.

## Using the manual

New members read Documents 1 to 8 in their first two weeks, following the checklist in Document 3. Documents 9 to 12 are references for later in the appointment. Document 13 is used at the end.

Units adapting the manual should replace K.R.L.-specific names, links, and meeting schedules, and check Documents 2, 4, and 7 against their own institutional requirements.
